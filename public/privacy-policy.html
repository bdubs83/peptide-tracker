Inner Circle Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Inner Circle handles information when you use the app. Inner Circle is a tracking and calculation support app for user-entered peptide, vial, schedule, reminder, inventory, and body-progress records.

Information we collect

Inner Circle can be used in local-only mode. If you choose to create an account, sign in, or use cloud sync, some data is transmitted off your device and stored with Firebase services. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Account information, such as email address, user ID, and display name
  • Peptide, vial, dose, schedule, cycle, reminder, inventory, and injection log records
  • Health and fitness records you enter, such as weight, body measurements, and notes
  • Files or document data you choose to save in the app, such as COA file information
  • App settings, sync status, timestamps, and device or installation identifiers used by Firebase or app services

How we use information

We use collected information to provide app functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Backing up, restoring, merging, and syncing your app data across your devices
  • Showing your schedules, reminders, calculations, inventory, and progress records
  • Maintaining app security, account access, and data integrity

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your app data for advertising or marketing purposes. We use service providers, including Google Firebase, to operate authentication, cloud storage, hosting, and related app infrastructure.

Local data and cloud sync

Data entered in local-only mode stays on your device unless you choose to sign in and use a cloud sync or backup feature. When cloud sync is enabled, selected app records are stored in your account copy so they can be restored or used on another device.

Security

Data transmitted between the app and Firebase services is encrypted in transit. We use access controls and account authentication to help protect cloud-synced records.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud-synced data at any time. Visit the Inner Circle account deletion page for instructions: Account and Data Deletion.

Children

Inner Circle is intended for adults. The app is not directed to children or minors under 18.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact peptide.app.support@gmail.com.
