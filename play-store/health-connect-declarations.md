# Health Connect permission declarations

Use the matching text below when Play Console asks how Inner Circle uses a Health Connect permission. Keep the submitted declaration aligned with the permissions in `android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`.

## Body composition

### Weight
Inner Circle reads user-authorized weight measurements to create or update the optional Body Tracker and show personal weight trends alongside the user's own peptide schedule. The app does not make medical recommendations or change doses from this data.

### Body fat
Inner Circle reads user-authorized body-fat measurements to create or update the optional Body Tracker and show personal body-composition trends. The app does not make medical recommendations or change doses from this data.

### Lean body mass
Inner Circle reads user-authorized lean-body-mass measurements to show an optional personal body-composition trend in Health Tracker. The data is visible only to the user in the app and is not used for medical recommendations or dose changes.

### Height
Inner Circle reads user-authorized height measurements to show an optional personal body-composition reference in Health Tracker. The data is visible only to the user in the app and is not used for medical recommendations or dose changes.

## Daily activity

### Steps
Inner Circle reads user-authorized step records to show an optional daily activity total and personal trend in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations or dose changes.

### Active calories burned
Inner Circle reads user-authorized active-calorie records to show an optional daily activity total and personal trend in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations or dose changes.

### Exercise
Inner Circle reads user-authorized exercise-session records to show an optional personal activity history in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations or dose changes.

## Recovery

### Resting heart rate
Inner Circle reads user-authorized resting-heart-rate measurements to show an optional personal recovery trend in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations, diagnosis, or dose changes.

### Sleep
Inner Circle reads user-authorized sleep-session duration to show an optional personal recovery trend in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations, diagnosis, or dose changes.

### Heart rate variability
Inner Circle reads user-authorized heart-rate-variability measurements to display an optional device-recorded recovery signal in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations, diagnosis, or dose changes.

### Blood oxygen saturation
Inner Circle reads user-authorized blood-oxygen-saturation measurements to display an optional device-recorded recovery signal in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations, diagnosis, or dose changes.

### Respiratory rate
Inner Circle reads user-authorized respiratory-rate measurements to display an optional device-recorded recovery signal in Health Tracker. The data is not used for medical recommendations, diagnosis, or dose changes.
